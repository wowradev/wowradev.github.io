03Process

How we work.

A short, transparent loop. No 80-page specs, no surprise invoices — just shipped software.

Discovery

We map the problem, the users, and the success metrics. You leave the first call knowing what we'll build and what it won't be.

Design

Fast prototypes in Figma, validated with real users. Visual language and interaction patterns locked before any line of code.

Build

Weekly demos, transparent backlog, clean code with tests. You see progress every Friday — no black boxes.

Launch & iterate

Production deploy, monitoring, post-launch tweaks. We stay until the product is healthy, then keep iterating with you.
