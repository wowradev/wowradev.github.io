Corgi Pipeline — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 August 2026 Effective date: 2 August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Corgi Pipeline application ("the Tool") handles information. The Tool is a private, first-party content publishing utility that uploads videos created by Wowra Dev to Wowra Dev's own TikTok account.

1. Who we are

  • Legal entity: Wowra Dev KFT
  • Registered address: HUNGARY BUDAPEST 1124 FODOR STR 57.
  • Contact e-mail: marcellkristof.elek@gmail.com

Wowra Dev KFT is the data controller for the processing described below.

2. What the Tool does

The Tool is an internal automation used exclusively by Wowra Dev to publish Wowra Dev's own videos to Wowra Dev's own TikTok account through TikTok's official Content Posting API. It is not offered to, installed by, or operated on behalf of any third party, and it does not interact with any TikTok account other than the account operated by Wowra Dev.

3. Data the Tool processes

  • TikTok authorization tokens — OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok after the account owner grants permission. Tokens are stored locally on Wowra Dev's own computer and are used solely to authenticate publishing requests to TikTok.
  • Basic TikTok account information — the account's open ID and display name returned by TikTok, used solely to confirm that publishing targets the correct account.
  • Video content and metadata — the video files, titles, and descriptions created by Wowra Dev that the Tool uploads to TikTok.

The Tool does not collect, process, or store any personal data of TikTok viewers or of any person other than the Wowra Dev account owner.

4. Data sharing and storage

Data is transmitted only to TikTok, via TikTok's official APIs, for the purpose of publishing content. No data is sold, shared with, or disclosed to any other third party. No analytics, advertising, or tracking services are used by the Tool. All credentials remain on Wowra Dev's own hardware.

5. Data retention and deletion

Authorization tokens remain stored locally until the account owner revokes the Tool's access (TikTok app: Settings → Security → Apps and services) or deletes the local configuration, either of which immediately ends the Tool's access to the account.

  • TikTok for Developers Terms: https://developers.tiktok.com/terms
